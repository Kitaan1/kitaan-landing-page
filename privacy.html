Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how Kitaan Reddy (ABN 38 808 734 344) handles your personal information. "We" and "us" mean Kitaan Reddy. We are the data controller of your information. We aim to handle it in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the other privacy laws that apply to you where you live.

What we collect

When you use the booking quiz or book a call: your name, phone number, email address, your quiz answers and your confirmation that you are 18 or over. Your quiz answers include your goals, what is holding you back, what you have tried and how you approach training.

If you become a client: information needed to coach you. This can include your bodyweight, measurements, food and calorie logs, training numbers, videos and photos, and anything you tell us about injuries or your health. Health information is sensitive information, so we only collect it with your consent and only when we need it to coach you.

Calls: sales and coaching calls may be recorded and transcribed using note taking tools such as Fathom or Otter. We will tell you before a call is recorded.

Website data: technical information such as your IP address, browser, device and the pages you visit, collected through the tools listed below.

Meta Pixel and tracking

Our home page uses the Meta Pixel, a tracking tool from Meta (the company behind Facebook and Instagram). If you visit from the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, it only runs if you press Accept on the tracking banner. Elsewhere it runs when the page loads, and you can turn it off at any time. When it runs, it records events such as visiting the page, starting the quiz, submitting the quiz and booking a call, and shares them with Meta along with technical data such as your IP address and browser information. Meta may match this to your Facebook or Instagram account. We use it to measure our ads and to show them to people who are more likely to be interested. Your name, phone number, email address and quiz answers are not deliberately sent to Meta by our quiz.

You can turn it off, or change your choice, at any time using the Tracking choices link at the bottom of the home page. You can also limit tracking through your Meta ad preferences, your browser settings or a tracker blocker.

Why we use your information

  • To contact you about your enquiry and arrange your call
  • To decide whether coaching is a good fit
  • To provide, review and improve your coaching
  • To run our business, including payments, records and advertising
  • To meet our legal obligations

We will only send you marketing emails or texts if you have agreed to receive them or the law otherwise allows it. Every marketing message will let you unsubscribe.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We use these service providers, who may process it for us:

  • Google (Google Sheets and Drive store leads and client programs, YouTube plays the page video once you press play)
  • Calendly (call booking)
  • Meta (Meta Pixel and advertising)
  • Cloudflare (website hosting)
  • Fathom and Otter (call recording and notes)
  • Skool (client community, if you join it)

Some of these providers are based in, or store data in, other countries, including the United States and Australia, so your information may leave your own country. Where the law requires it, we rely on safeguards such as the standard contractual clauses our providers offer. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Under 18s

Our coaching and booking form are for people aged 18 or over. If you tell us you are under 18, the quiz will not ask for your contact details. If you believe we hold information about a person under 18 without proper consent, please contact us and we will deal with it.

Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and keep it only for as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law. No online system is completely secure.

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask to access or correct the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it.

If you are in the EU, EEA or UK

We rely on these legal bases: your consent (Meta Pixel and health information), the steps needed to enter into or perform a contract with you (your call and coaching), our legitimate interests (running and improving our business) and legal obligations. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your information, to receive a portable copy, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier use. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are in Canada

You can ask to access or correct your information and withdraw your consent, subject to legal limits. You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your provincial regulator.

If you are in the United States

Some states give residents rights to access, correct or delete personal information, including consumer health data. We do not sell your personal information. Email us to make a request.

Complaints

If you have a privacy complaint, please email us first and we will try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
